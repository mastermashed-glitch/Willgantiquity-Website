Mission, Method, Pricing & Policy

  • The Mission: Save and provide quality antiques for customers seeking to purchase a piece of history and art of their own.
  • The Method: Authentically restoring pieces using various techniques without ruining the patina. Pieces in the showroom will show a Restored badge showing which piece was restored and how.
  • Pricing: Pricing is based off of recently sold listings and local/online market prices. I believe in offers so fair offers and bundle deals are considered!
  • AI Policy: Initial pictures of items will NOT be enhanced by AI in anyway with the exception of the last picture in the carousel showing a staging use concept if needed.

Want to Work Together?

Part of what I do is offer a service to help go through and assess items (Ex: A loved ones home items or assets left behind) and help sell or give direction on ways to make money for clients and their families. There are no upfront costs! Profits for each item sold is split 50/50 after platform fees and taxes.

1. Non-Destructive Stripping

When beneficial, a good cleaning with mineral spirits, ultra sonic bath, and or delicate paint stripping apply

2. Mechanical Revival

Mortise locks, Architectual Salvage, lamps, etc are all inspected, rewired or lubricated on most pieces. Vintage electronics are verified working before being listed unless specified otherwise

3. Period-Honest Finishes

I like to preserve original warm age patina and style while protecting surfaces for decades to come—using hand applied clear wax, linseed oils, and appropriate oil based rust preventatives.
